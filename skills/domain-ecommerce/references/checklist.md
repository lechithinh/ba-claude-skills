# E-commerce BA checklist — questions to ask / Câu hỏi BA TMĐT phải hỏi

## Business model (ask first)
- [ ] Single-brand store, marketplace, omnichannel, or B2B?
- [ ] Channels: web, app, social commerce, external marketplaces?
- [ ] Own warehouse, platform warehouse, or seller ships?

## Cart & checkout
- [ ] Guest checkout allowed?
- [ ] What if price/promotion/stock changes between cart and placing the order?
- [ ] Minimum order value? Max quantity per SKU/customer?
- [ ] Delivery zones & address format (province/district/ward after administrative changes)?
- [ ] Delivery date estimate shown? From which data?

## Promotions & vouchers
- [ ] Which promotions/vouchers can stack? (ask for a stacking matrix)
- [ ] Discount allocation to items — needed for partial returns & invoices?
- [ ] Voucher restored on cancellation/return?
- [ ] Budget cap reached mid-campaign — what does the customer see?
- [ ] Fraud: multiple accounts abusing new-user vouchers?

## Payment
- [ ] Payment methods per channel/order value? COD limits?
- [ ] Payment timeout and reservation release?
- [ ] Payment success but order not updated (lost callback) — reconciliation?
- [ ] Refund methods & timelines per payment type?

## Orders & fulfillment
- [ ] Order status list and who/what triggers each transition?
- [ ] Cancellation rules per status and per actor (customer/seller/admin/system)?
- [ ] Split order and partial shipment handling — how does the customer see it?
- [ ] Stock reservation timing and expiry? Oversell policy?
- [ ] Failed delivery attempts and RTS handling?

## Returns
- [ ] Return window, excluded categories, evidence required?
- [ ] Who pays return shipping per reason?
- [ ] Refund before or after inspection?
- [ ] Exchange supported or only refund?

## Marketplace (if applicable)
- [ ] Seller verification documents?
- [ ] Commission & fee structure; settlement cycle & hold period?
- [ ] Seller SLAs and penalties?
- [ ] Dispute between buyer and seller — who decides?

## Non-functional (e-commerce-specific)
- [ ] Peak events (11.11, 12.12, Tết, flash sales): expected concurrent users & orders/minute?
- [ ] Inventory consistency under concurrency (no oversell)?
- [ ] Page speed targets for product listing/detail (SEO & conversion)?
- [ ] Analytics events & attribution (UTM, affiliate)?
- [ ] Personal data & marketing consent?

## Stakeholders to involve (often forgotten)
Merchandising/Category · Marketing/CRM · Operations/Warehouse · Customer service · Finance/Accounting (invoice, reconciliation) · Legal · Logistics partners · Seller management (marketplace).
