# Regulatory touchpoints (topics to ask about)

> **Last reviewed:** 2026-11 · **Status:** community draft, needs review by a lending compliance expert.
> This file lists **topics to raise with Compliance/Legal**, not legal conclusions or citations. Consumer lending rules (rates, fees, conduct, reporting) change and differ by licence type: always verify the current regulation and the lender's licence conditions.

| Topic | What a BA should check |
|---|---|
| Interest rate and fee rules | Any caps, how fees count toward cost, what must be disclosed |
| Pre-contract disclosure | Information, effective rate, repayment schedule shown before signing |
| Consumer protection and collection conduct | Allowed contact hours and frequency, third-party collection rules, complaint handling |
| Credit information | Bureau inquiry consent, reporting to the bureau, accuracy and dispute handling |
| Debt classification and provisioning | Groups, DPD criteria, restructuring effect |
| eKYC and identification | Remote verification requirements and limits |
| Personal data protection | Consent, purpose, sharing with partners/collectors, retention |
| Electronic contracts and signatures | Legal validity and evidence |
| AML | Customer due diligence for lending |
| Insurance and bundled products | Rules on mandatory or bundled insurance sales |

## Turn into requirements
Add compliance requirements with owner, evidence and sign-off date; keep rates, caps, windows and contact rules as **parameters**; version the disclosure texts and record which version each customer saw.
