# Lending BA checklist / Câu hỏi BA phải hỏi

## Product and pricing
- [ ] Product type (cash, installment, BNPL, line), channel, secured or not, funding source?
- [ ] Amount and term ranges; **interest method (flat or reducing)**; fees (origination, insurance, late, early repayment) and disclosure wording?
- [ ] Is the effective annual rate calculated and shown? Which formula and rounding?

## Origination and decision
- [ ] Eligibility rules and knock-outs? Required documents by segment?
- [ ] Bureau consent and inquiry timing; behaviour when bureau is down?
- [ ] Score cut-offs, auto-approve/review/reject bands, **approval authority matrix**?
- [ ] eKYC mismatch, retry limits, fraud flags, duplicate or repeated applications?
- [ ] Offer validity, customer can change amount/term? Resume incomplete application?

## Contract and disbursement
- [ ] E-sign method, evidence kept, contract version?
- [ ] Disbursement account validation, timeout, **signed but not disbursed**, partial disbursement, fee deduction?

## Servicing and collections
- [ ] Schedule rules (due date, holiday, rounding)? Allocation order? Overpayment?
- [ ] Auto-debit retries and customer notification? Missed payment communication limits?
- [ ] DPD buckets and actions; contact hours and frequency limits; promise-to-pay handling?
- [ ] Early/partial repayment recalculation; payoff quote validity?
- [ ] Restructuring policy and effect on reporting?

## Risk, compliance, non-functional
- [ ] Reports to bureau and regulators, provisioning inputs?
- [ ] Personal data retention, consent records, audit trail fields?
- [ ] Peak events (campaigns, month-end due dates), availability, batch windows for interest accrual?

## Stakeholders often forgotten
Risk and credit policy · Collections · Compliance/Legal · Finance (provisioning, accounting) · Operations · Customer support · Partners/merchants (BNPL) · IT Security.
