# Regulatory touchpoints (topics to ask about)

> **Last reviewed:** 2026-11 · **Status:** community draft, needs review by a logistics/legal expert.
> This file lists **topics to raise with Legal/Compliance**, not legal conclusions or citations. Rules for transport, postal services and e-commerce delivery change: always verify the current regulation and the licences of each partner.

| Topic | What a BA should check |
|---|---|
| Postal / courier service licensing | What activities need a licence; partner licences |
| Restricted and dangerous goods | Prohibited items, packaging, declaration, inspection rights |
| Personal data of recipients | Consent, purpose, sharing with couriers, retention, masking |
| COD handling and cash management | Limits, record keeping, remittance responsibilities |
| Consumer protection | Delivery time promises, refusal and return rights, complaint handling |
| Transport rules | Vehicle, driver and working-hour rules (own fleet) |
| E-invoice and tax | Invoicing of shipping fees and COD fees |
| Customs (cross-border) | Documents, duties, restricted items |
| Insurance and liability | Limits of liability, claim windows |

## Turn into requirements
List compliance requirements with owner and sign-off date; keep restricted-item lists, limits and windows as **configurable parameters**.
